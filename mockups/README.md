# Kiamora GUI Mockups

Each interface mockup is supplied in editable SVG and rendered PNG. Source photography used by the mockups is under `assets/`.

## Storefront

- `storefront-home-desktop` — complete 1600×3600 editorial homepage with carousel, brand manifesto, category discovery, signature collection, featured products, assurance, consultation CTA and footer.
- `storefront-home-mobile` — 430×2800 mobile interpretation with a deliberately recomposed hero and touch-friendly hierarchy.
- `storefront-home-carousel-slide-02` — second carousel state demonstrating a distinct story, right-aligned copy and active-dot change.
- `storefront-collection-desktop` — professional collection/catalogue screen with hero, filters, sorting, product grid and saved-item affordances.
- `storefront-product-colour-selection` — upgraded product detail page with Yellow, White and Rose Gold selector. The Rose Gold choice updates the gallery, price/configuration and enquiry state.

## Administrative portal

- `admin-dashboard` — catalogue metrics, content-health checks, recent products and quick actions.
- `admin-homepage-carousel` — structured carousel management, responsive media/focal points, validation, scheduling behaviour and live preview.
- `admin-product-colour-images` — colour variants, multiple images per colour, primary image, alt-text readiness and publication controls.
- `admin-users` — multiple administrator accounts, roles, statuses, invitation flow and last-sign-in visibility.

The user-provided visual direction is retained under `reference/`. The Kiamora concept photography under `assets/` is original mockup imagery, not approved catalogue inventory. Use `UI_SPECS.md` for interaction, accessibility, responsive and Angular component requirements. Mockup content is illustrative and must not be treated as approved production data or customer claims.
